### Nebula — Cluster Management System

Nebula is a proposed cluster management and coordination layer designed to extend Pulsar beyond individual engine deployments allowing multiple Pulsar instances to operate together as a unified distributed system.

**Nebula is currently a concept and is not under active development.** Its implementation has not started as our current priority is to make Pulsar itself stable and useful to the developer community.

Interested in understanding the concept? See [Nebula — Cluster Management System](./NEBULA.md) for its detailed architectural description.

You can also explore the Nebula Concept Preview on our website for an [early look at the vision](https://pulsar-engine.vercel.app/).
